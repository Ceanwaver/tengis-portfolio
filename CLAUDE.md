# Tengis Ulzii — Portfolio

Single-file portfolio site: everything (HTML, CSS, JS, base64 photo) lives in `index.html` — except case-study images, which load lazily from `assets/<project>/` (Kuul: phone screens as PNG resized from `kuul-mockups/`, wordmark and app-icon SVGs from the Kuul brand folder; Tino: the user's screens in `assets/Tino/<service>/`, resized for the web into `assets/Tino/web/`, plus 3D icons from `~/Documents/Tino/Tino-3D-ICONS/Final Version` in `assets/Tino/web/icons/`).

- Concept: a crate of vinyl records, inspired by https://a24.raviklaassens.com/ — every project is a bare 3D vinyl record (no sleeve), currently pressed in its brand colour. No intro; the work is the first screen.
- Tab: "CEAN (Tengis Ulzii)" with a "Cn." icon in bold Geist (drawn on a canvas, since a favicon can't load web fonts): `favicon.svg` is just the letters, filling the icon, and holds a black and a white PNG switched by `prefers-color-scheme`; `favicon-64.png` (white on a black tile) is the fallback for browsers without SVG icons, and the 180 px `apple-touch-icon.png` is for the iPhone home screen.
- Style: clean and modern, light mode by default (`data-theme="light"`), optional dark (`data-theme="dark"`, remembered in localStorage).
- Fonts: Instrument Serif (titles), Geist (text), Geist Mono (labels) via Google Fonts.
- Crate (`#work`): a sticky stage. A port of A24's disc gallery (https://a24.raviklaassens.com/), read from their source; the constants below are A24's.
  - **Camera:** 40° fov at 4.4·U (U = R, A24 lengths × U). frame() eases to the deck's 26° framing (`deckZ`) as the turntable slides in.
  - **Desktop layout (>991px):** records lie on an arc inside a gallery frame `galQ` turned −30° on X and −30° on Y, scaled by 1.08.
    - Offset m = i − L, θ = 0.35m: x = 5.52·sinθ, z = 2.4(1−cosθ), yaw −θ.
    - The active record is lifted 0.06 and at scale 1; the others are max(0.8, 1−0.12|m|).
    - Overlap comes from real 3D separation; `renderOrder` follows depth.
  - **Touch/tablet layout (≤991px):** a straight line. `galS` is fitted so the neighbours' centres sit on the screen edges; the active record is ×1.5 and passed records turn 60°.
  - **Motion:** one shared position `crate.L` drives every record, which keeps them in sync.
    - Steps (`stepTo`) tween L on A24's cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1): wheel 0.51s; keys min(0.6, 0.42+0.05·dist); clicks clamp(0.45+0.11·dist, 0.46, 1.05); touch/scrollbar 'settle' 0.56s; cancel 'snap' 0.46s quartic.
    - Each record follows its target with a lag, k = clamp((drag ? 15 : 11) − 1.15|m|, 6.5, 15). This is what makes the S-curve smooth.
    - `crateTick` writes the page scroll while it runs. A tween keeps the records inside the crate but eases the page from where it really is (`crate.y0`), so a settle that starts in About never jumps.
    - Touch and the scrollbar scroll natively; `snap(y, dir)` settles them (`armSettle`, re-armed when the last finger lifts). A touch stops a running step only once the finger actually moves (>6px). Gliding into the crate from About lands exactly on the end record; a push that stepped onto an end record has its momentum swallowed.
    - Opening a record settles the crate onto it (`stepTo(i, 'snap')`). Jumps (index pick, hash, Next wrapping last → first) set `landNext`, so records land instead of flying. Resizing keeps the crate on its record (phones ignore toolbar-only height changes).
    - Past either end the page scrolls on to About/Contact.
  - **Turn toward travel:** the shared velocity `ce` (kicked by each step) drives:
    - lean = clamp(±√|ce|·0.055, ±0.3), a yaw toward the direction of travel (×1.12 on non-active records);
    - a bank of −0.32·lean;
    - a dip of min(0.09, 0.014|ce|).
  - **Wheel (`wheelPush`, A24's step machine):** a step once 6px builds up; the rest of that push and its momentum are swallowed until a pause (≥130ms), a fresh notch, a rising flick, or a held scroll (~480ms). Sideways swipes step too. ← → step one record.
  - **Pointer (mouse only, on `.stage`):**
    - The hovered record tilts up to 0.21 rad toward the cursor (its place on the canvas), smoothed twice.
    - A fast pass nudges the record it lands on; a click dents it (the `push` spring).
    - Clicking the front record opens it; clicking another brings it to the front.
    - Dragging the front record turns it over in your hand (`flip` group, `softSpin`, back upright over 1.2s).
    - Dragging elsewhere moves the crate (left = next, 5.7 records per canvas width), then coasts (friction 0.89) and springs onto a record. A drag never counts as a click.
  - **Turntable flight:** opening a record makes the others shrink and recede (A24's flight); poses follow instantly while it runs.
  - Records and their labels hold still in the crate; only the record on the turntable spins. Perfectly still at rest.
  - Top-left credits table (Role / Year / Platform / Team) and two key results update for the active record.
  - Light: a soft key-light glint (`drawGlint`, clipped to the disc) and the rainbow sheen band slide with each record's real turn away from rest (lean, hover, flip), on top of the studio reflections.
- Clicking a record (or opening `#<slug>`) adds `html.is-detail`: the crate and UI clear, a turntable glides in from the bottom-right, the record floats to a point just above the platter (turning flat on the way, `dLift`), then lowers straight down onto the spindle (`dDrop`) — never passing through the deck — and the tonearm swings over; it turns slowly (~13 rpm, slower than a real 33⅓). Closing plays it in reverse. The `#detail` overlay shows the title/credits over the turntable and the story (summary, results, problem/process/outcome with mockup placeholders) below. "← All projects" or Esc closes it.
- 3D: Three.js 0.160 via importmap (jsDelivr) — records built from lathes (radial UVs) with a groove/track-gap strip texture, anisotropic highlights, raised lip, a canvas centre label per project, and a fixed rainbow sheen band (additive overlay that doesn't spin). Turntable is minimal and procedural: a dark walnut slab (photographed grain + normal + roughness maps generated with Magnific, embedded as `#wood-color/-normal/-rough` data URIs), satin aluminium platter under a black felt mat, a slim steel tonearm with matte black counterweight and headshell. Lighting is a custom studio environment (dark room + big softboxes, `studio()`), NoToneMapping. Falls back to a flat label image without WebGL.
- Coloured vinyl (on, testing): `COLOURED = true` presses each record — and its All projects thumbnail — in its project's `label` colour and flips the centre label (ink-coloured paper, title in the brand colour); `false` = classic black vinyl. Pinecone (#000000) and Playmo (#1F1F1F) stay black either way.
- Logo labels (Garage: its G tile): a project with `logo: '<id>'` uses that image (embedded as `#logo-<id>`) as its centre label instead of the printed title; Kuul uses its glasses logo (label colour #5B4BFF).
- Stickers: a project can have `stickers: [{ id, at (degrees round the record), r (distance from centre), size, turn }]`; images are embedded as `#sticker-<id>` data URIs (Kuul has clock, megaphone, pencil, magnifier, paper). They're pressed flat on the vinyl (turn with the record) and drawn on the All work thumbnail too.
- Projects live in the `PROJECTS` array in the module script (title, role, year, platform, team, label/ink colours, results). Kuul is first; 8 projects, Khanbogd last (TDB Bank was removed from the crate — it stays in the About experience list). `[bracketed]` values are placeholders.
- All projects (`#index`, `html.is-index`, opened from the "All projects" nav link or `#all`): a grid of every project as a flat record thumbnail (`drawThumb`) with title, category, year and role; picking one opens it on the turntable. Below it, "All solutions": a numbered list of smaller solutions from the `SOLUTIONS` array (title, text, for, year; `buildSolutions`) — hidden for now (`SHOW_SOLUTIONS = false`). Close/Esc returns.
- Case studies: a project with `story` (an array of blocks rendered by `storyHTML`: `hero`, `text`, `features` (screen + title + text), `list`, `brand` (idea, process steps, wordmark tiles, app icons, colours), `numbered`) replaces the placeholder Problem/Process/Outcome under the turntable. Kuul's is `KUUL_STORY`, written from `kuul-portfolio.md` and the Kuul logo book; role Co-founder & CDO. Tino's is `TINO_STORY`, from `content/tino.md` (project copy is written in `content/<slug>.md` first), with extra blocks: `hero` with `screens` (on the page, no panel), screens shown on a real iPhone 15 Pro rendered in Blender (Cycles): `tools/iphone/iphone.py` models the phone to Apple's dimensions (70.6 × 146.6 × 8.25 mm, continuous-curvature corners, contoured black-titanium rail, inset glass with a 1.7 mm black border, Action/volume/side buttons, antenna lines, the front camera lens in the Dynamic Island), puts each screenshot on the display (emission under a clear coat, Standard view so colours stay exact), stands it on a seamless floor/wall shadow catcher (lean 5°, facing forward) and writes `assets/Tino/web/phones/<screenshot-name>.webp` plus one shared `shadow.webp` (shadow faded before the frame edges). Re-render with `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup -P tools/iphone/iphone.py -- tools/iphone/tino.json` (~15 s a phone on the M4). On the page `phone(src)` emits `.phone` = the phone's footprint inside the 1280×1780 render (`--fx0/--fy0/--fw/--fh` from the render's framing), with the shadow layer (opacity 0.32, stays put) and `.phone__body` (the render + a glare masked to it) that `initPhones`/`tiltTick` lean toward the cursor (±11° / ±8°, eased like the records), `chapter` (big title + 3D icon), `cards` (Tino Go service types with 3D cars), `icons` (two drifting rows of the 3D icon set), `showcase` (a mini-app's text beside its screens, straight on the page with no backdrop — the `bg` values are unused; screens are cropped to phone proportion from the top; optional `photos`), `strip` (a row of captioned screens on a brand-coloured panel; currently unused). COP17 is the last `showcase` item (icon: its app tile, `iconTile`), screens from `assets/Tino/COP-17/`. Showcase screens are large (each up to 31% of the panel, max 290px), and `numbered` with `intro`/`after`. Garage's is `GARAGE_STORY`, from `content/garage.md` (expanded copy plus a researched Digital Garage brief with sources): app screens from `assets/Garage/E-Commerce App` rendered on the Blender iPhone (`tools/iphone/garage.json` → `assets/Garage/web/phones/`), and a `web` block for the desktop products (B2B, Forum & Blog, Admin, Company Landing in a light browser window; POS on plain tablet screens). The Garage app screenshots have no Dynamic Island, so their renders use `"island": true` (a black pill drawn under the lens). The `web` block also has a `tv` variant (16:9, thin black bezel); MKaraoke's is `MKARAOKE_STORY` (from `content/mkaraoke.md`): a `tv` hero and `tv` web items with its screens as 1600-wide JPEGs in `assets/Mkaraoke/web/`; research (journey maps, user flow) and the design system (wireframe + style guide cropped from the case study) as `boards` in `web/ds/`. The Garage record's label is the orange G app tile cropped from the company landing page (`#logo-garage`, 161 px; swap for the real logo when available). On the project page the wheel glides with a soft delay (`dGlide`, rate 5 — ~70% in 0.25s, ~90% in 0.45s; touch/keys native) and texts and assets rise and fade in as they scroll into view (`.rv` → `.in` via `revealer`/`armReveal`, items entering together stagger 90ms; images also scale up slightly).
