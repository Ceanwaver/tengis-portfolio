# Tengis Ulzii — Portfolio

Single-file portfolio site: everything (HTML, CSS, JS, base64 photo) lives in `index.html` — except case-study images, which load lazily from `assets/<project>/` (Kuul: phone screens as PNG resized from `kuul-mockups/`, wordmark and app-icon SVGs from the Kuul brand folder; Tino: the user's screens in `assets/Tino/<service>/`, resized for the web into `assets/Tino/web/`, plus 3D icons from `~/Documents/Tino/Tino-3D-ICONS/Final Version` in `assets/Tino/web/icons/`).

- Concept: a crate of vinyl records, inspired by https://a24.raviklaassens.com/ — every project is a bare 3D vinyl record (no sleeve), currently pressed in its brand colour. The way in (`#intro`, black and white, built on the Cn. mark): C, n and the dot slide up into place in white on black while a hairline under them fills and a mono counter runs 000→100 (real load: fonts + page, min ~2 s, max 6 s); then C and n slide out and the dot opens into a circle (a radial mask on `.intro__load`) onto the site, which waits blurred and greyscale behind a gate — a serif line, a round "Enter with sound" button with sound bars (that click unlocks Web Audio and calls `sfx.enable(true)`) and "Continue without sound". Entering plays the same iris again: the gate's words and buttons fade, and the button pressed opens into a circle (the gate's own radial mask, `iris()` shared with the dot, 1.1 s) onto the site in full colour. While `html.is-gated`, wheel/touch/keys don't reach the crate. Then the work is the first screen.
- Tab: "CEAN (Tengis Ulzii)" with a "Cn." icon in bold Geist (drawn on a canvas, since a favicon can't load web fonts): `favicon.svg` is just the letters, filling the icon, and holds a black and a white PNG switched by `prefers-color-scheme`; `favicon-64.png` (white on a black tile) is the fallback for browsers without SVG icons, and the 180 px `apple-touch-icon.png` is for the iPhone home screen.
- Link previews: Open Graph + Twitter tags in the head point at `og.jpg` (1200×630: name, role and the eight record thumbnails from `drawThumb`, composed on a canvas in the page). Re-make it when the projects or their colours change.
- Sound (`sfx`): off by default; "Enter with sound" or the Sound switch turns it on (remembered). No music (the user tried per-record songs, an original piano piece and a Pixabay track, then removed music). A calm underwater ambience plays under everything while sound is on (`startAmbient`/`stopAmbient`, generated live, inspired by indigo-laboratory.it's sound): a low D drone (D2 A2 D3 as steady sines — an earlier drifting triangle drone sounded too "vibrating"), brown-noise current gently rising and falling, all through a low-pass (~900 Hz) that breathes only slightly into a long dark reverb (`depth`, 4 s); a soft wind behind it (band-passed stereo noise, its band slowly sweeping, rising from silence over ~25 s to gain 0.03; its gusts come from the crate's wind lines via `sfx.gust`); scheduled details — a muffled chord swelling in every 9–16 s, bubble clusters every 4–10 s, a distant bell every 12–22 s, a deep slow call every 25–45 s; fades in over 5 s to gain 0.16 and out over 2 s. A project can have its own room under the ambience while its page is open (`SCENES` in `sfx`, `sfx.scene(slug)` from `openDetail`/`closeDetail`; fades in over 3 s, out over 2 s): Kuul, made for students, gets a quiet coffee shop with young people talking at the next table — a real recording (Pixabay, freesound_community "Quiet cafe, chatter"), cut to a calm minute without the milk frother, its end crossfaded into its start, as `assets/sfx/cafe.m4a` (AAC 64 kbps, ~470 KB), looped at gain 0.3 through a 3.8 kHz low-pass with a little room. Tino, a super app for everyone in the city, gets a busy street: Pixabay's "Street city traffic voices busy London" (freesound_community; people walking past, talking, cars), cut to the first 45 s before a horn, as `assets/sfx/city.m4a`, plus synthesised ads from a distant shop speaker every 14–28 s (`advert`: a ding-dong PA chime or a bouncy square-wave jingle in a random key, band-passed 700–2600 Hz, panned to one side; a scene's `extra`/`every`). Garage gets a workshop: a 16 s auto-shop bed (`assets/sfx/garage/bed.m4a`, from Pixabay's "Auto shop soundscape") and, every 8–18 s, one recorded moment at a random spot in the stereo field, never the same twice in a row (a scene's `shots`, played under its bus so they fade with it): a car starting and idling, a door opening and shutting, a ratchet on a bolt, an impact wrench (`engine/door/ratchet/impact.m4a`, trimmed mono clips from Pixabay; total ~350 KB). Pinecone (its summer UI/UX classes) gets a classroom: a 25 s bed of a teacher lecturing with a class murmuring under it (`assets/sfx/pinecone/class.m4a`, Pixabay's "Science teacher lecturing" + "Classroom background" mixed at 0.12), and every 10–20 s chalk on the board or a voice from the room speaking up (`chalk`, `voice1–3`, phrases cut by level from Pixabay's "Lecture"; not checked by ear). Cheepah (shopping) gets a store at work: a 54 s supermarket-checkout bed (`assets/sfx/cheepah/store.m4a`, Pixabay's "Supermarket") and every 6–14 s products being scanned (`scan1/2`, from "Retail scanner"), groceries going into a paper bag (`bag`, `rustle1/2`) or the till opening with a ding (`till`). Playmo (TV streaming) gets a film playing (no real film, which would be copyrighted): a 42 s bed of a TV's muffled voices and score (`assets/sfx/playmo/film.m4a`, Pixabay's "TV playing in the next room") and every 10–20 s a dramatic moment or a cinematic boom from the film (`moment`, `hit`, kept quiet), an audience laughing (`laugh`) or popcorn (`popcorn1/2`). MKaraoke gets a karaoke night: a good singer, on purpose not a real karaoke recording (those are off-key amateurs and often covers of copyrighted songs) but an original royalty-free song, The_Mountain's "Pop Vocal" (Pixabay Music), its first 104 s with a bar's chatter mixed under it and its loud end crossfaded into its quiet intro (`assets/sfx/mkaraoke/room.m4a`, ~840 KB), and every 14–26 s the room cheering or reacting (`cheer`, `react1/2`). Khanbogd (mining) gets a mine's site office: a 21 s bed of people talking plans over (Pixabay's "People talking from distant", up front) with the pit at work under it ("Mining ambience"; `assets/sfx/khanbogd/office.m4a`), and every 10–20 s an excavator (`dozer`), a truck's reversing beeps (`beep`), a dump truck's air brakes (`truck`) or a radio crackle (`radio`). Goes Green (tree planting) gets nature: a 60 s bed of wind in the trees with a swell of wind through grass mixed in (`assets/sfx/goes-green/wind.m4a`, Pixabay's "Wind in trees" + "Wind rustling grass"), and every 8–16 s a gust through the leaves (`gust`), a branch swinging (`branch1/2`), a tree creaking (`creak1/2`) or birds and insects (`birds`). Every project now has its own room. The UI sound kit is switched off for now (`UI_SOUNDS = false` in `sfx`; every one-shot checks `ui()`), only the ambience and the project rooms play. A warm, soft UI sound kit in the spirit of C418's Minecraft music (the user's reference was Aria Math; the glassy kit before it felt off), synthesised, nothing copied: round synth plucks (two detuned triangles + a quiet sawtooth through a quickly closing low-pass, `pluck`) and soft swelling triangle pads (`pad`) on a mid D-major pentatonic (`PENTA`, D5–D6) in a dark 1.8 s room: a pluck on link/button clicks, a faint one on hover (links, buttons, cards, and a crate record via a MutationObserver on `.records.is-hover`; mouse only, ≥70 ms apart), falling plucks over a soft chord in `closeDetail` (opening a record and the needle landing are silent, at the user's request), a warm welcome chord when sound comes on, two notes up/down for the theme switch, a low pluck on the deck's buttons, plucks for Kuul's stickers. The original piano piece "Undertow" is kept, unused, in `tools/music/undertow.js` (Salamander samples in `assets/piano/`, local only). A project with an empty `team` hides the Team row (Kuul, Cheepah).
- Style: clean and modern, light mode by default (`data-theme="light"`), optional dark (`data-theme="dark"`, remembered in localStorage).
- Fonts: Instrument Serif (titles), Geist (text), Geist Mono (labels) via Google Fonts.
- Crate (`#work`): a sticky stage. A port of A24's disc gallery (https://a24.raviklaassens.com/), read from their source; the constants below are A24's.
  - **Camera:** 40° fov at 4.4·U (U = R, A24 lengths × U). frame() eases to the deck's 26° framing (`deckZ`) as the turntable slides in.
  - **Desktop layout (>991px):** records lie on an arc inside a gallery frame `galQ` turned −30° on X and −30° on Y, scaled by 1.08.
    - Offset m = i − L, θ = 0.35m: x = 5.52·sinθ, z = 2.4(1−cosθ), yaw −θ.
    - The active record is lifted 0.06 and at scale 1; the others are max(0.8, 1−0.12|m|).
    - Overlap comes from real 3D separation; `renderOrder` follows depth.
  - **Touch/tablet layout (≤991px):** a straight line. `galS` is fitted so the neighbours' centres sit on the screen edges; the active record is ×1.5 and passed records turn 60°.
  - **Motion:** one shared position `crate.L` drives every record, which keeps them in sync.
    - Steps (`stepTo`) tween L on A24's cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1): wheel 0.51s; keys min(0.6, 0.42+0.05·dist); clicks clamp(0.45+0.11·dist, 0.46, 1.05); touch/scrollbar 'settle' 0.56s; cancel 'snap' 0.46s quartic.
    - Each record follows its target with a lag, k = clamp((drag ? 15 : 11) − 1.15|m|, 6.5, 15). This is what makes the S-curve smooth.
    - `crateTick` writes the page scroll while it runs. A tween keeps the records inside the crate but eases the page from where it really is (`crate.y0`), so a settle that starts in About never jumps.
    - Touch and the scrollbar scroll natively; `snap(y, dir)` settles them (`armSettle`, re-armed when the last finger lifts). A touch stops a running step only once the finger actually moves (>6px). Gliding into the crate from About lands exactly on the end record; a push that stepped onto an end record has its momentum swallowed.
    - Opening a record settles the crate onto it (`stepTo(i, 'snap')`). Jumps (index pick, hash, Next wrapping last → first) set `landNext`, so records land instead of flying. Resizing keeps the crate on its record (phones ignore toolbar-only height changes).
    - Past either end the page scrolls on to About/Contact.
  - **Turn toward travel:** the shared velocity `ce` (kicked by each step) drives:
    - lean = clamp(±√|ce|·0.055, ±0.3), a yaw toward the direction of travel (×1.12 on non-active records);
    - a bank of −0.32·lean;
    - a dip of min(0.09, 0.014|ce|).
  - **Wheel (`wheelPush`, A24's step machine):** a step once 6px builds up; the rest of that push and its momentum are swallowed until a pause (≥130ms), a fresh notch, a rising flick, or a held scroll (~480ms). Sideways swipes step too. ← → step one record.
  - **Pointer (mouse only, on `.stage`):**
    - The hovered record tilts up to 0.21 rad toward the cursor (its place on the canvas), smoothed twice.
    - A fast pass nudges the record it lands on; a click dents it (the `push` spring).
    - Clicking the front record opens it; clicking another brings it to the front.
    - Dragging the front record turns it over in your hand (`flip` group, `softSpin`, back upright over 1.2s).
    - Dragging elsewhere moves the crate (left = next, 5.7 records per canvas width), then coasts (friction 0.89) and springs onto a record. A drag never counts as a click.
  - **Turntable flight:** opening a record makes the others shrink and recede (A24's flight); poses follow instantly while it runs.
  - **Wind lines** (`#windlines`, a 2D canvas behind the transparent WebGL canvas, crate only — faded out on a project and on All projects, paused off-screen, off with reduced motion): thin, straight ink-coloured lines rising to the upper right at 14–18° (the user tried curly, level and wavy ones; alpha 0.3 light / 0.34 dark) drawn in from the head and erased from the tail, faded at both ends. About 2.5 a second drift by (at most 40 at once) with the steady wind; every 10–20 s a gust (rise 2–4 s, settle 3–5 s) brings a flurry of quicker, longer ones and calls `sfx.gust(up, peak, settle)` to swell the ambience's wind on the same curve, so what you hear and see move together (the ambience no longer times its own gusts).
  - Records and their labels hold still in the crate; only the record on the turntable spins. Perfectly still at rest.
  - Top-left credits table (Role / Year / Platform / Team) and two key results update for the active record.
  - Light: a soft key-light glint (`drawGlint`, clipped to the disc) and the rainbow sheen band slide with each record's real turn away from rest (lean, hover, flip), on top of the studio reflections.
- Sticker splash (Kuul only for now): a project's `splash` list (`{ id, x, z (deck units; platter centre −1.4, 0.3), size, turn, h (arc height), flip, spin }`) bursts out from under the record the moment it touches the platter (`detail.d` ≥ 0.84): each sticker arcs out tumbling (`buildSplash`/`poseSplash`, staggered, 1 s), lands flat on the deck, clear of the buttons and fader, with one small hop and a soft shadow; closing pulls them back under in 0.4 s.
- Clicking a record (or opening `#<slug>`) adds `html.is-detail`: the crate and UI clear, a turntable glides in from the bottom-right, the record floats to a point just above the platter (turning flat on the way, `dLift`), then lowers straight down onto the spindle (`dDrop`) — never passing through the deck — and the tonearm swings over; it turns slowly (~13 rpm, slower than a real 33⅓). Closing plays it in reverse. The `#detail` overlay shows the title/credits over the turntable and the story (summary, results, problem/process/outcome with mockup placeholders) below. "← All projects" or Esc closes it.
- 3D: Three.js 0.160 via importmap (jsDelivr) — records built from lathes (radial UVs) with a groove/track-gap strip texture, anisotropic highlights, raised lip, a canvas centre label per project, and a fixed rainbow sheen band (additive overlay that doesn't spin). Turntable is procedural, a classic DJ deck (no brand): a brushed-silver top plate (`silver`, anisotropic) on a black rubber base with big rubber feet, a platter with four rows of strobe dots on its rim (canvas texture) that turns with the record, under a black felt mat, a red strobe lamp + `PointLight` at the front left, a start/stop button and two speed buttons (plain, the first one's LED lit), a pitch fader with a printed scale and a green zero light on the right, and an S-shaped silver tonearm (`TubeGeometry`) with a banded counterweight, black headshell with finger lift, arm rest, cue lever and anti-skate dial. (The old walnut deck and its Magnific wood maps are in git history.) The buttons (start/stop and two speed buttons, no printed legends) just press — a sink and a clack, no function; the pitch fader just drags along its slot (snaps to zero, where the green light shows) and changes nothing — the record and its song always play at their own speed. Clicks reach the deck through `#detail` (the hero lets them through) and are ray-cast against the buttons and knob once the deck is in place; each record starts at 33, running. A project can swap the fader knob for its own little 3D model with `fader: '<name>'` (built by `faderArt[name]`, sitting in the slot and dragged the same way): Tino's is `car`, its blue Tino Go car (rounded blue body, glossy black glass dome, white front light bar, black wheels with grey hubs, mirrors), front facing up the slot. Lighting is a custom studio environment (dark room + big softboxes, `studio()`), NoToneMapping. Falls back to a flat label image without WebGL.
- Coloured vinyl (on, testing): `COLOURED = true` presses each record — and its All projects thumbnail — in its project's `label` colour and flips the centre label (ink-coloured paper, title in the brand colour); `false` = classic black vinyl. Pinecone (#000000) and Playmo (#1F1F1F) stay black either way.
- Logo labels (Garage: its G tile): a project with `logo: '<id>'` uses that image (embedded as `#logo-<id>`) as its centre label instead of the printed title; Kuul uses its glasses logo (label colour #5B4BFF).
- Stickers: a project can have `stickers: [{ id, at (degrees round the record), r (distance from centre), size, turn }]`; images are embedded as `#sticker-<id>` data URIs (Kuul has clock, megaphone, pencil, magnifier, paper). They're pressed flat on the vinyl (turn with the record) and drawn on the All work thumbnail too.
- Projects live in the `PROJECTS` array in the module script (title, role, year, platform, team, label/ink colours, results). Kuul is first; 9 projects, Goes Green last (TDB Bank was removed from the crate — it stays in the About experience list). `[bracketed]` values are placeholders.
- All projects (`#index`, `html.is-index`, opened from the "All projects" nav link or `#all`): a grid of every project as a flat record thumbnail (`drawThumb`) with title, category, year and role; picking one opens it on the turntable. Below it, "All solutions": a numbered list of smaller solutions from the `SOLUTIONS` array (title, text, for, year; `buildSolutions`) — hidden for now (`SHOW_SOLUTIONS = false`). Close/Esc returns.

- Sections below: About (photo, bio, experience, skills, tools), Contact. In Contact the cursor draws one smooth calligraphic line (a canvas `.contact__ink` under the text, inspired by dkton.at's footer but cleaner): a pen tip eases after it, the last 0.75 s of its path is drawn as joined quadratic curves, thicker where you move slowly and thinner where you flick, tapering and fading toward the tail with a small nib dot at the tip; ink colour follows the theme (`--ink`), it only animates while there's a line, and it's off with reduced motion.
