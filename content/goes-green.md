## Summary

Goes Green is a tree-planting app for Oyu Tolgoi's employees. Every employee plants a tree, and the app lets them look after it from then on, so a single planting day grows into a lasting commitment to a greener Mongolia.

## The problem

Planting a tree is the easy part; keeping it alive is the hard part, especially in Mongolia's dry climate. Corporate planting days often end the moment the last tree is in the ground, and the trees are quickly forgotten. Goes Green set out to keep employees connected to the trees they planted and responsible for their care, long after the event itself.

## What Goes Green does

- **My trees:** every tree an employee plants gets its own page: its species, height, trunk diameter, the day it was planted, and its growth so far.
- **Care:** employees log each watering, and the tree's care history shows at a glance; a tree that wasn't looked after is marked as dead.
- **Lands:** each planting site shows its trees on a little map, how many of them are thriving, and the site's overall progress.
- **Events:** company planting events appear on the home screen, with a countdown to the next one.
- **Points and ranks:** planting, caring for trees and joining events earn leaf points, and points raise an employee's rank.
- **Shop:** points can be spent on Oyu Tolgoi merchandise, such as the limited-edition Goes Green T-shirt.
- **Learn:** articles about Mongolia's nature, each with a short quiz that earns points.
- **Together:** company and personal statistics, including progress toward Oyu Tolgoi's goal of 100 million trees.

## My role

- Designed the app on my own, end to end: onboarding, home, trees and lands, the care log, statistics, points and ranks, the shop and articles.
- Created the visual style and the design system for the app.

## The challenge

**From one day to a habit.** The real challenge was turning a one-off planting event into something employees return to. The app had to make each tree feel personal, theirs and alive, so that looking after it feels rewarding rather than like a chore.

**A reason to come back.** Points, ranks, a shop and quizzes give employees small rewards for every watering and every event, so caring for a tree has a payoff long after planting day.

**Simple enough for everyone.** Oyu Tolgoi's employees range widely in age, role and comfort with technology. Every flow had to be clear and quick, so anyone could check on their tree and take care of it in a few taps.
