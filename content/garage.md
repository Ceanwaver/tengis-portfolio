## Summary

Garage.mn is Digital Garage’s car-parts and car-care ecosystem, from a store that only shows parts that fit your car to the tools that run the business.

## About Digital Garage

Digital Garage (Цахим Дижитал Гараж LLC) has been working since 2020 with a team of automotive and IT engineers. Its aim is to bring Mongolia's car-parts and car-service market online, a market where a single car can have 20,000 to 30,000 different parts, each with its own code and standard.

- **Digital Garage app (May 2024):** enter your car's plate number and see only the parts that fit it. No more guessing, wasted trips or wrong parts. The app also flags manufacturer recalls and technical upgrades a car needs, which owners can have done free of charge at official dealers. This was built in partnership with Mongolia's National Center for Transport.
- **Garage.mn:** the web store, with 10,000+ products and delivery in 2 to 24 hours.
- **Garage B2B (May 2025):** a wholesale marketplace where workshops and resellers, including those in the countryside, order parts from many suppliers in one place, pay, settle up and arrange delivery. Official distributors such as Forward Motors sell through it, and the company is working with banks such as Golomt Bank on credit and collateral-free loans for auto-service businesses.
- **Garage Forum and Blog:** a community where drivers ask questions, share experience and learn about their cars.
- **Tools for the business:** apps for mechanics, inventory, warehouses and the counter.

## The problem

When I joined, Garage's products had grown one by one, each with its own patterns and its own rough edges. Finding the right part for your car was harder than it should be. The people behind the store, from mechanics and warehouse staff to cashiers, had tools that didn't work together. And with B2B, POS and new apps on the way, there was no shared foundation to build them on. The business needed a consistent experience for customers and a connected set of tools for everyone running it.

## My role

I led design at Digital Garage across the entire ecosystem, from the customer-facing store to the internal tools.

- Started with a UX audit of the existing products, mapping the problems that cost users time and the company sales.
- Built the foundational design system and wrote its guide: colour, type, spacing, iconography and a component library, with rules for how they're used. Every Garage product now builds on it.
- Redesigned the e-commerce website and the mobile app based on the audit.
- Designed new products from scratch: B2B e-commerce, the POS, the admin, the Mechanic app and the Inventory app.
- Designed the Digital Garage company landing page and the Garage forum and blog.
- Polished the web apps in detail, screen by screen and state by state, so they feel finished and consistent rather than assembled.
- Worked closely with engineering and the business teams to take each product from research to release.

## The challenge

Garage wasn't one product but a whole ecosystem, and every part of it served a different person. A driver hunting for the right brake pads, a mechanic logging a finished job, a warehouse worker counting stock, a cashier serving a queue and a business buying parts in bulk all needed something different. Each product had to be designed around its own users and the way they actually work, from a relaxed browse on a phone to a fast, repetitive task at a counter. At the same time, all of them had to feel like one family. That's what the design system made possible.

## Design system

**What it is:** The foundation every Garage product is built on, from the customer app to the POS.
**What I did:** Defined the foundations (Garage orange, a neutral scale, type, spacing, radii and icons), built a component library in Figma and wrote the guide that explains how and when to use each piece.
**Design focus:** A system flexible enough for a relaxed shopping app and a fast shop-floor POS alike, so every product looks and behaves like one family and new screens can be built quickly.

## Projects

### 1. E-Commerce App

**What it is:** The Garage shopping app for iOS and Android.
**How it works:** Add your car by plate number or model once, and the whole catalogue (parts, oils, accessories) shows only what fits. Browse categories and brands, book auto repair and services, and check out in a few taps. A subscription unlocks member prices and perks.
**Design focus:** Shopping for car parts on a small screen: the plate-number search right at the top, results that always fit your car, and a checkout built for one hand.

### 2. B2B E-Commerce

**What it is:** Garage B2B, the wholesale marketplace for workshops, resellers and auto businesses.
**How it works:** Businesses sign up, browse a catalogue searchable by OE number, part number or manufacturer, see their own pricing and stock, place large orders, and pay and settle in one place.
**Design focus:** Designing for professional buyers who order often and in volume: dense but readable product lists, quick quantities and reordering, and a payment flow businesses can trust.

### 3. POS

**What it is:** The point-of-sale system for selling parts at the counter, on a tablet.
**How it works:** Cashiers search by part number, OE number or product name, filter by the customer's car, build the cart and take payment.
**Design focus:** Fast and error-proof at the counter: big touch targets, the customer's car always in view, and a cart summary that keeps the queue moving.

### 4. Forum & Blog

**What it is:** The Garage community: a forum and blog where drivers ask questions, share experience and read about cars, parts and maintenance.
**How it works:** Members pick the topics they care about at sign-up, follow discussions and reply. The blog publishes guides and stories.
**Design focus:** Making expert knowledge easy to find and easy to join in with: readable articles, simple posting and replying, and natural links from a discussion to the right parts in the store.

### 5. Admin

**What it is:** The back office where the Garage team manages the platform.
**Design focus:** A calm, data-dense workspace built on the same design system, with clear navigation and dashboards that put the important numbers first.

### 6. Company Landing

**What it is:** The website of Digital Garage, the company behind every Garage product.
**What I did:** Designed the company site: the mission, the team, the partners and the product family with its users.
**Design focus:** Presenting an automotive-tech company with confidence: a dark, warm visual language with Garage orange, and the whole ecosystem shown as one family of products.

### Also designed

- **Garage Mechanic app:** for mechanics to see incoming jobs, track their work and log what they've completed. Large, clear actions for hands-on people in a workshop.
- **Garage Inventory app:** for tracking stock, receiving deliveries and finding where each part is stored. Fast lookups and flows that make mistakes hard to make.
