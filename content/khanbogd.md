## Summary

Khanbogd Iraeta is a Mongolian steel manufacturer, with its factory in Khanbogd in the Gobi and its head office in Ulaanbaatar. It produces grinding balls, flanges, special steel and precision parts for mining, energy and heavy industry. I designed the company's website, [khanbogdiraeta.com](https://www.khanbogdiraeta.com/), and the design system behind it.

## The problem

Khanbogd Iraeta sells to some of the most demanding industries there are, and to partners such as Oyu Tolgoi and Gestamp. Its website had to work as a serious business tool: present complex industrial products clearly, show international-standard quality, and speak to both Mongolian and international clients, while still looking modern and confident.

## My role

- Designed the company website end to end, in Mongolian and English.
- Structured the site around how industrial buyers look for information: About, Applications, Products, Projects, Advantages, Partners, News and Contact.
- Created the design system: colour, type, spacing and components, so every page feels consistent and new content is easy to add.

## The challenge

**Making heavy industry clear.** Grinding media, forged balls, pipe flanges and wind turbine flanges are technical products with detailed specifications. The challenge was presenting them so that an engineer or buyer can quickly find the right product, its applications and its specifications, without the site turning into a dense catalogue.

**Building trust at first sight.** For an industrial supplier, credibility is everything. The design puts the proof up front: ISO 9001:2015 certification, decades of manufacturing and export experience, real projects and trusted partners.

**Two languages, one system.** The site works fully in Mongolian and English. The design system was built to handle both, with layouts and components that stay balanced whichever language is shown.

## Outcome

- A bilingual company website that presents Khanbogd Iraeta's products, projects and quality to clients in Mongolia and abroad.
- A design system that keeps the brand consistent online and makes the site easy to grow.
