## Summary

Tino is the super app for everyday life in Ulaanbaatar: one place to pay, ride, shop, charge your phone and more, used by almost 200,000 people.

## The problem

Everyday life in Ulaanbaatar was spread across a pile of separate apps: one for rides, one for payments, others for groceries and tickets, each with its own login and its own wallet. For visitors it was harder still, with no single place to get a SIM card, a map and a ride. Tino set out to bring all of it into one app, without letting it turn into a cluttered menu of everything.

## What's inside

One account and one wallet for the services people rely on every day, with new mini-apps joining as the ecosystem grows.

- Payments and the Tino wallet.
- Tino Go: rides with real-time tracking and trip sharing.
- Groceries with same-day delivery.
- Tino Charge: powerbanks to rent from stations in cafés, malls and offices.
- Smart Fridge: scan a QR code, take a snack, pay automatically.
- NBA Fantasy: build your own NBA team and compete, right inside Tino.
- Donation: support causes and people in need in a few taps.
- COP17: a dedicated mini-app for COP17's attendees.
- Lottery: buy tickets and follow the draws in the app.
- Tino Teen: parental controls that let teens use Tino while parents stay in charge.
- eSIM, tickets, Tino Activity and Tino AI.

## The challenge

The hardest part of Tino was never a single screen. It was fitting many products, each with a completely different purpose, into one app that still feels simple.

**Many mini-apps, one product.** Rides, deliveries, payments, groceries, charging, tickets, fantasy sports and more each come with their own logic, their own steps and their own edge cases. I had to think through every one of them flow by flow, designing each journey on its own terms, while making sure they all belong to the same app and connect through one account and one wallet.

**A different user behind every service.** The person booking a taxi on the way to work is not the person sending a parcel across town, planning a company bus trip, landing at the airport for the first time in Mongolia, or picking a fantasy lineup before tip-off. Each service has its own audience, with its own needs, context and level of patience. So I designed each flow around its own users, rather than forcing everyone through one generic pattern.

**Keeping it whole.** The real challenge was holding both ideas at once: giving every service the experience its users need, while keeping Tino coherent, familiar and easy to move through as one product.

## Featured: Tino Go

Tino Go is the flagship of the super app: the service people open most, and the one I'm proudest of designing.

**What it is:** Premium ride-hailing in Ulaanbaatar with professional drivers, built for locals and visitors alike. It runs as two connected products: the rider experience inside Tino, and a dedicated Tino Go app for drivers. Inside, Tino Go offers eight service types, and each one has its own UX, designed around how and when people actually need it.

**How it works for riders:**
1. Set a pickup point and a destination.
2. See the ride and the fare, and confirm.
3. Follow the driver live on the map until they arrive.
4. Share the trip with someone you trust while you ride.
5. Pay automatically from the Tino wallet when the ride ends.

**How it works for drivers:** A separate app where drivers go online, accept requests, navigate to the pickup and the destination, and track their trips and earnings.

### Service types

Every service type shares the same foundation (map, driver card, live tracking, wallet payment), but each one has a flow shaped around its own job.

#### 1. Main Taxi

**What it is:** The everyday ride: a car to where you need to go, right now.
**UX:** Pickup and destination, fare and confirm, then live tracking until arrival.
**Design focus:** The fastest path in the app. It's the default service, so the most common trip takes the fewest taps.

#### 2. Delivery

**What it is:** A driver picks up a package and brings it across the city.
**UX:** Pickup and drop-off addresses, sender and receiver contacts, a note about the item, then live tracking of the package instead of a ride.
**Design focus:** Shifting the flow from "where am I going" to "where is my package going", with both the sender and the receiver kept informed.

#### 3. Pre-Book

**What it is:** A ride scheduled ahead for a set date and time.
**UX:** Choose the date and time first, then the route; the booking is confirmed in advance and the rider is reminded before pickup.
**Design focus:** Building confidence in a ride that hasn't happened yet: a clear confirmation, a reminder, and an easy way to change or cancel.

#### 4. On-Demand Driver

**What it is:** A professional driver who drives your own car.
**UX:** Set where the driver should meet you and where you're going in your car, then follow the driver's arrival as with any ride.
**Design focus:** Making it immediately clear that this is a driver for your car, not a car with a driver, so expectations match from the first screen.

#### 5. Car Rental with Driver

**What it is:** A car and a driver booked for hours or a whole day.
**UX:** Choose the car type, the start time and the duration instead of a single destination, then see the price for the whole booking.
**Design focus:** Moving from a per-trip mindset to a time-based booking, with pricing by duration that's easy to understand.

#### 6. Bus Rental

**What it is:** A bus with a driver for groups, events, company trips and tours.
**UX:** Choose the bus size by number of passengers, then the date, route and duration, and send the booking.
**Design focus:** Group trips that need more detail than a taxi, kept as simple as possible to request.

#### 7. Airport Pickup

**What it is:** A ride from or to Chinggis Khaan International Airport.
**UX:** Enter the flight details and the arrival time; the driver meets you on arrival and takes you straight to your destination.
**Design focus:** A calm first moment in the country for visitors: booked before landing, with a clear meeting point and the driver's details ready.

#### 8. Global Taxi (Splyt)

**What it is:** Taxis abroad, booked through Tino via Splyt's global ride-hailing network.
**UX:** The same Tino Go flow, used in other countries: set the route, see local options and the fare, and ride with a local partner.
**Design focus:** Making a partner network on another continent feel exactly like home: same screens, same wallet, no new app to download.

### Design focus across Tino Go

- **Speed:** booking a ride takes only a few taps from the moment Tino opens.
- **Clarity in motion:** a live map, driver details and the arrival time that stay readable at a glance, in a moving car or on a busy street.
- **Safety first:** live tracking and trip sharing on every ride, always one tap away.
- **One system, two sides:** the rider and driver apps speak the same visual language, so both sides of every trip see the same status at the same moment.
- **Seamless payment:** no cash and no card step at the end; the Tino wallet settles the fare automatically.

## Graphic design: 3D service icons

Beyond the product design, I also created the graphics. Every Tino Go service type has its own 3D icon that I designed, so each service is recognisable at a glance from the home screen.

**The set:** Main Taxi, Delivery, Pre-Book, On-Demand Driver, Car Rental with Driver, Bus Rental, Airport Pickup and Global Taxi.

**The style:** One locked house style keeps the set feeling like a family:
- the same camera for every icon, almost front-on, turned slightly to the right and seen from a little above;
- smooth, satin surfaces with soft, friendly forms;
- no drop shadows, so the icons sit cleanly on any background, light or dark;
- square artwork that fills the frame, so every icon reads at the same size in the grid.

**Why it matters:** Eight services with eight different flows need to be told apart instantly. The icons do that job before a single word is read, and they give Tino Go a warm, tactile character that sets it apart from flat, generic ride-hailing apps.

## Web design: Tino landing page

I also designed Tino's landing page at [tino.mn](https://tino.mn), the front door for anyone meeting Tino for the first time.

**The goal:** Explain a super app in seconds. Tino does many things, so the page had to show that range without overwhelming people, and lead every visitor to download the app.

**The structure:**
- **Hero:** one clear promise, "Your Super App for Everything", with App Store and Google Play buttons right away.
- **One section per service:** Smart Fridge, Tino Charge, Tino Go and Grocery Shopping, each with a short pitch and simple numbered steps showing how it works.
- **More inside:** eSIM, payments and the mini-apps that keep arriving, grouped together so the page stays focused.
- **Support:** a How to Use guide and a News page for people who want to go deeper.

**Design focus:**
- The same visual language as the app, so the website and the product feel like one brand.
- Each service explained in a few simple steps, so a visitor understands it before even installing the app.
- A clear path to download from every part of the page.

## Services

Every service lives inside one Tino account and pays from one Tino wallet. Each brief covers what the service is, how it works, and the design challenge it solved. (Tino Go is featured above.)

### Payments & Tino Wallet

**What it is:** The core of the app. One wallet that pays for every service inside Tino, plus everyday payments outside it.
**How it works:** Top up once and pay for a ride, a grocery order, a powerbank or a snack without entering card details again.
**Design focus:** Making one wallet feel safe and obvious across a dozen very different services: clear balances, instant confirmations and a payment step that looks and behaves the same everywhere.

### Grocery Shopping

**What it is:** Fresh groceries and household essentials delivered the same day.
**How it works:** Browse the catalogue, add items to the cart, choose a delivery slot, and check out with in-app payment.
**Design focus:** A catalogue that's fast to browse on a phone, a cart that's easy to review, and a checkout where picking a delivery slot feels effortless.

### Tino Charge

**What it is:** Powerbank rental from stations in cafés, malls and offices across the city.
**How it works:** Scan the QR code on any Tino Charge station to release a powerbank, charge on the go, and return it to any station. The first minutes are free; after that you pay only for the time you use.
**Design focus:** A scan-to-unlock flow that works in seconds, a live timer and cost so there are no surprises, and a map for finding the nearest station to return it.

### Smart Fridge (Tino Store)

**What it is:** Smart fridges with snacks and drinks in offices, gyms and public spaces, restocked daily and open 24/7.
**How it works:** Find a nearby fridge, scan its QR code, open the door, take what you want and close it. Payment happens automatically.
**Design focus:** A checkout-free experience: a single scan, clear guidance at the fridge, and a receipt that arrives automatically once the door is closed.

### NBA Fantasy

**What it is:** A fantasy basketball mini-app inside Tino, where fans build their own team from real NBA players and compete as the season plays out.
**How it works:** Pick your players, set your lineup, and earn points from how they perform in real games, then climb the leaderboard against other fans. [Adjust to the real rules: budget, leagues, prizes]
**Design focus:** Bringing a game into a utility app. It had to feel exciting and alive, with lineups, live scores and rankings, while still sitting naturally next to rides and payments inside the same product.

### Donation

**What it is:** A donation mini-app inside Tino that makes it easy to support causes, campaigns and people in need.
**How it works:** Choose a cause, pick or enter an amount, and give in a tap from the Tino wallet. [Adjust to the real flow: causes, recurring giving, receipts]
**Design focus:** Building trust and warmth. Each cause is presented clearly and honestly, and giving takes only a few taps, so a good intention turns into action without friction.

### COP17

**What it is:** A dedicated mini-app for COP17, the UN Convention to Combat Desertification conference hosted in Ulaanbaatar in 2026, built for COP17's attendees.
**How it works:** Everything an attendee needs for the conference in one place, so they can focus on the sessions, not on getting around.
- **Before the conference:** a live countdown to the opening, a preparation checklist, and nearby places to stay and eat, recommended through Tino.
- **The programme:** the full programme by day and zone (Blue and Green), searchable, with a personal agenda and clear warnings when two sessions overlap.
- **Getting around:** a venue map with halls and zones, with Tino taxi, shuttle and activity options a tap away.
- **My COP:** one profile for the visit: the green passport, the agenda, payments and receipts, language and help.
- **After the event:** a personal recap of each attendee's COP17 journey.
**Design focus:** A welcoming first experience for attendees from around the world, many of them using Tino for the first time. Clear English, simple navigation and fast access to the services they need most, so attendees can focus on the conference, not on getting around.

### Lottery

**What it is:** A lottery mini-app inside Tino, where people can buy tickets and follow the draws without leaving the app.
**How it works:** Choose a draw, pick your numbers or let the app pick them, pay from the Tino wallet, and check the results when the draw is announced. [Adjust to the real flow: draws, prizes, how winners are notified]
**Design focus:** Capturing the excitement of a draw while keeping everything clear and trustworthy: transparent rules, a simple purchase, tickets that are easy to find again, and results that are unmistakable.

### Tino Teen (Parental Control)

**What it is:** A mini-app that lets parents set up and manage Tino for their teenagers, giving teens their own access to Tino's services while parents stay in control.
**How it works:** A parent links their teen's account to their own, then sets limits and permissions and follows activity from their side. [Adjust to the real features: spending limits, allowed services, top-ups, activity history]
**Design focus:** Designing for two users at once. Parents need clear, reassuring controls they can understand at a glance; teens need an experience that feels like their own, not like being watched. Balancing safety with independence shaped every screen.

### eSIM

**What it is:** Mobile data plans you can buy and install straight from the app, without a physical SIM card.
**How it works:** Choose a plan, pay from the Tino wallet, and install the eSIM on your phone.
**Design focus:** Turning a technical setup into a short, guided flow that works for visitors arriving in Mongolia as well as for locals.

### Tino Ticket

**What it is:** Tickets for events and more, bought and stored in the app.
**How it works:** Find an event, choose your tickets, pay in the app, and show them at the door from your phone.
**Design focus:** A quick purchase flow and tickets that are easy to find on the day, even in a crowd at the entrance.

### Tino Maps

**What it is:** A city map built into the app, powered through Tino's partnership with GrabMaps.
**How it works:** Search places across Ulaanbaatar, see them on the map, and jump straight into a ride or a service nearby.
**Design focus:** A map that ties every service together: rides, charging stations, fridges and places to go, all in one view.

### Tino Activity

**What it is:** A guide to nightlife, food, events and lifestyle in the city.
**How it works:** Discover what's on, see the details, and book or buy tickets in a few taps.
**Design focus:** An editorial, visual browsing experience that turns discovery into action, connecting straight to tickets, rides and payments.

### Tino AI

**What it is:** An AI assistant built into the app.
**How it works:** Ask in chat for help with Tino's services, recommendations or questions about the city.
**Design focus:** A conversational interface that feels natural, and answers that lead straight into the right service inside the app.
