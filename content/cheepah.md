## Summary

Cheepah brings Chinese online shopping to Mongolia: millions of products from China's marketplaces, in a Mongolian app, with fast delivery and no hidden fees. Together with one teammate, I designed the app over four months, creating a Mongolian version of a Chinese e-commerce experience that feels modern, clean and easy to trust.

## The problem

Mongolians have long shopped from China's huge online marketplaces, but getting there was a hassle. The platforms are in Chinese, payment is complicated, and getting a parcel home meant agents, cargo companies and extra fees that were hard to predict. Cheepah set out to remove all of that: one app in Mongolian, prices you can understand, and delivery handled from order to doorstep.

## What Cheepah solves

- **Language:** products and the whole shopping experience in Mongolian, instead of Chinese.
- **One place to shop:** browse and buy from China's marketplaces without leaving the app.
- **Clear costs:** no hidden fees, so the price you see is the price you pay.
- **Delivery handled:** fast shipping from China to Mongolia, without arranging cargo or agents yourself.
- **Peace of mind:** Cheepah's own promise is "Сэтгэл амар, тааламжтай шоппинг", a calm and pleasant way to shop.

## The challenge

**Calm out of chaos.** Chinese e-commerce apps are famously dense: every screen is packed with text, prices, badges, banners and product images. My main challenge was keeping the richness and choice that make these platforms work, while making each screen feel modern, clean and easy to read. That meant constant decisions about hierarchy: what a shopper needs to see first, what can wait, and what can go.

**A design system that could keep up.** A shopping app of this size quickly produces a huge number of screens. To keep them consistent, the design system had to be highly structured from the start, with clear rules for colour, type, spacing and layout that held across every part of the app.

**A component library people could understand.** With so many screens came a lot of components: product cards, price tags, badges, filters, banners and more. I put a great deal of work into turning them into a clear, well-organised component library, so that anyone on the team, designer or developer, could find the right piece and use it correctly.
