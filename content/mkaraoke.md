## Summary

MKaraoke, from Mmusic, brings karaoke to the living room: a Smart TV app where your phone becomes the remote. I designed it at Pinecone Studio.

## The problem

At karaoke most of the time is lost choosing songs: flipping through a printed booklet that never has enough hits. MKaraoke set out to make karaoke easy and fun anywhere, with songs picked and queued from your own phone.

## The challenge

**Your phone as the remote.** Scan the QR code on the TV and your phone becomes the controller. The remote still works for everything, with song codes and a keyboard in Mongolian and English.

**Every kind of TV.** Lyrics and controls readable from ten feet away, inside the safe area, on any screen, indoors or out.

**Ads that don't kill the vibe.** Banners placed between songs, never over the lyrics, so the party keeps going.

## Research: what singers told us

We surveyed 76 active Mmusic users about their karaoke nights.

1. **Karaoke anywhere.** Most go 1–2 times a quarter for 1–3 hours, and want to sing more often, with anyone.
2. **Help to start.** Choosing the first song is hard, so the app suggests warm-up songs.
3. **More visual.** Music videos and visuals behind the lyrics, not random videos.
4. **Praise, not scores.** Only 30% wanted their singing rated, so the app cheers instead.

**Personas and journeys:** Two personas, one using the TV remote without the app and one downloading it, showed where the night goes wrong: signing up and searching for songs. The user flow gets both to the first song as quickly as possible.

## Design system

Wireframes first, then a style guide: colours, Manrope type, a grid and a spacing scale.

## Screens

### 1. Getting started

**What it is:** The splash, sign-in and the QR screen that pairs a phone with the TV.
**Design focus:** Getting from the sofa to the first song in as few steps as possible.

### 2. Picking songs

**What it is:** Warm-up suggestions to start the night, and search by name, code or artist when the queue is empty.
**Design focus:** Fast choices with a remote: a few big options, song codes, and a keyboard built for arrow keys.

### 3. Singing

**What it is:** The player with the music video and lyrics, the countdown before a song, and the screen between songs.
**Design focus:** Lyrics first, controls out of the way, and the queue always visible at the top.

## What we learned

- Designing for TV was new to us, so we checked every design on a real TV to see it as viewers would.
- Indoors and outdoors, with a phone or a remote: so many situations made us look at the product from every angle.
