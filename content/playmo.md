## Summary

Playmo is a Mongolian streaming service with Hollywood films, K-dramas, Asian series and local titles, all with Mongolian subtitles or dubbing. I designed the Playmo experience for the big screen, its Smart TV app, and created the design system and component libraries behind it.

## The problem

Playmo was built for phones, but people watch films and series on the biggest screen in the house. A TV is a completely different place to design for: it's viewed from across the room and controlled with a remote that has only a few buttons. Playmo needed a TV app that feels natural from the sofa, with browsing and watching as effortless as on a phone.

## My role

- Created Playmo's design system: colour, type, spacing, focus states and motion, set up for viewing at a distance.
- Built the component libraries: posters, rows, banners, buttons, menus and player controls, documented so the team could build with them consistently.
- Worked with the development team to bring the designs to the screen. [Adjust: team size, how you worked]

## The challenge

**Designing for the sofa, not the hand.** On a TV there's no touch and no mouse, only a remote with arrow keys and an OK button. Every screen had to be designed around focus: it should always be obvious where you are, where you can go next, and what pressing OK will do. Moving through a large catalogue had to take as few clicks as possible.

**Readable from across the room.** Type, posters and controls all had to work at a distance of several metres, on screens of every size. That meant bigger type, stronger contrast and calmer layouts than on mobile.

**A system made for TV.** Patterns from mobile don't simply carry over to a television. I built a design system and component libraries specifically for the big screen, with focus states, rows, cards and player controls defined once and reused everywhere, so the app stays consistent and new screens are quick to build.
